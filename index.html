How to Use Grace Personal Finance Tracker

Step-by-Step Guide
1

Enter Your Monthly Income & Salary

Go to the Income & Outgoings tab. Enter your annual gross salary in British Pounds (£). The financial engine automatically calculates your estimated UK Income Tax (20%/40%) and National Insurance contributions to derive your net take-home salary. You can also add freelancing or side hustle income.

2

Add Your Monthly Outgoings & Expenses

Click + Add Item in the outgoings table to log recurring expenses (e.g. Rent, Utilities, Groceries, Transport, Entertainment). Select whether the item is Fixed (essential) or Variable. All totals update live on your main Dashboard.

3

Schedule Financial Events in Calendar

Switch to the Event Calendar tab to log one-off future expenses (e.g., Summer Holiday £400, Car Maintenance £200) or extra income bonuses. The calendar grid visualizes upcoming dates and projects your net accumulated cash position over time.

4

Export Formula-Driven Excel & PDF Reports

Visit the Export tab to download your data.
• Interactive Excel (.xlsx): Exports a live spreadsheet with dynamic Excel formulas (`=SUM()`, `=B2-B10`). You can open it in Microsoft Excel, change your salary inside Excel, and watch all figures automatically recalculate!
• PDF Report: Generates a high-resolution printable presentation document.

5

Cloud Auto-Save (Firebase Database)

When you are logged into your account, every budget modification automatically syncs to your **Firebase Cloud Database**. Your data stays saved so you can log back in from any device without losing your progress.
