How to Use Grace Personal Finance Tracker

Step-by-Step Guide
1

Enter Your Monthly Income & Salary

Go to the Income & Outgoings tab. Enter your annual gross salary in British Pounds (£). The financial engine automatically calculates your estimated UK Income Tax (20%/40%) and National Insurance contributions to derive your net take-home salary.

2

Add Your Monthly Outgoings & Expenses

Click + Add Item in the outgoings table to log recurring expenses (e.g. Rent, Utilities, Groceries, Transport, Entertainment). Select whether the item is Fixed (essential) or Variable.

3

Schedule Financial Events in Calendar

Switch to the Event Calendar tab to log one-off future expenses or extra income bonuses.

4

Export Formula-Driven Excel & PDF Reports

Visit the Export tab to download dynamic Excel sheets with real formulas (`=SUM()`, `=NetPay - Outgoings`).

Save & Share Your Financial Data

Export your complete financial records either as an interactive, formula-driven Excel sheet or a clean PDF report.
